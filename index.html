Our Philosophy

Where Coffee Science Meets Culinary Passion

AURA was born out of a relentless desire to elevate coffee from a daily routine to a sensory ritual. We partner directly with micro-lot farmers across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Panama to ensure ethical sourcing and impeccable roasting standards.

Our all-day bistro kitchen complements every cup with hand-crafted sourdough pastries, truffle-infused dishes, and botanical mocktails prepared fresh daily.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We welcome guests daily from 8:00 AM until 1:00 AM. Breakfast & brunch menu is served until 5:00 PM.

Is table reservation mandatory?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we strongly recommend booking in advance for weekend evenings and larger groups via WhatsApp (01025134834).

Do you sell whole roasted coffee beans?

Yes! All our single-origin micro-lots are available in 250g bags, roasted weekly and sealed for peak flavor freshness.
